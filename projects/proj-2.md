Created a custom test board for the battery management system of Cornell Racing's ARG25 race car. I designed, brought up, and tested with this board to ensure pack voltage was being correctly read with striking accuracy. I worked with +120V in a high voltage section of the board, as well as low voltage in voltage dividers.
